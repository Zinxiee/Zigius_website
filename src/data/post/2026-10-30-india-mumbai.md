Hello World!

I draft this whilst sitting in Goa (my stop after Mumbai) watching the sunset!

On my first day in Mumbai, I met Jay who travels the world cutting and styling hair on cruise ships! He introduced me to his friend Yasin who works at the hostel.

![](~/assets/images/173705.jpg)

Jay took me partying that night and I demonstrated why I shouldn't ever be allowed to dance. We had great food and I had my first trip in an Auto Rickshaw (tuk tuk) 🛺 

![](~/assets/images/173703.jpg)

![](~/assets/images/173731.jpg)

![](~/assets/images/173730.jpg)

The next day, Jay took me to Colaba on an old British train that crosses Mumbai. Our stop was called Churchgate, if that wasn't a dead giveaway ;)

On the way we saw cricket games and I was introduced to sugar cane juice (AMAZING).

We visited the famous Leopold cafe on Colaba Causeway, where I got a falooga, a very creamy dessert drink. 

![](~/assets/images/173726.jpg)

We then passed the Taj Mahal Palace (which is different to the Taj Mahal) and visited the Gateway Of India. 

![](~/assets/images/173729.jpg)

![](~/assets/images/173728.jpg)

In the early hours of the morning one night I was unpacking my bags on the floor in the dark when one of my dorm mates tripped over me. It was my fault really, I should have had a torch on.

But that's how I met Senthil. A wild and funny guy who knew his way around Bandra West. He invited me to a theatre show that night at the Chimbai Village Theatre, and we were joined by others from the hostel.

No spoilers, but the show titled "No Clear Rule" had a good twist, that in truth I should have seen coming!

![](~/assets/images/173722.jpg)

![](~/assets/images/173723.jpg)

![](~/assets/images/173721.jpg)

We then went for dinner and then onto the Irish bar!

![](~/assets/images/173719.jpg)

The following day we explored Bandra West, which is apparently the "place to be" in Mumbai and Senthil showed me the best place to get a Sitaphal milkshake (a custard apple milkshake).

On my way back, I accidentally found my way into a "Bombay Zine Fest" and learnt what a Zine was - a type of magazine that is made by a single person or small group. It was an amazing event and I'm so glad I stumbled upon it.

From there I had dinner with the gang and then it was time to catch my sleeper bus to Goa!

![](~/assets/images/173708.jpg)

![](~/assets/images/173706.jpg)

I should mention, it was my friend Alfiya who recommended Mumbai as my first stop (she's biased because she was born there 😉), but it was still a great first stop on my travels across India!

Catch you next time!
