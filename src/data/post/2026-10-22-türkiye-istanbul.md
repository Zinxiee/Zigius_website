Hello World!

I write this as I fly out to Mumbai from Istanbul, 

I was a little optimistic in my last post, we didn't necessarily make it to Istanbul that same day.

Because of the storm over Istanbul, we ended up playing a game of hokey pokey between the European and Asian continents. 

We ended up in Izmir and then commuted up.

![](~/assets/images/screenshot_20261017_015817_maps.png)

Istanbul hadn't been hit with this much rainfall in over 80 years, so it wasn't just our plane that was rerouted.

![](~/assets/images/screenshot_20261017_015804_maps.png)

We arrived at our hostel ≈23.5 hours after setting off from the UK, which is a rocky start but hey ho, we made it!

Our stay was at Lola Backpackers Hostel, and this here is Lola:

![](~/assets/images/20261016_102523.jpg)

And this is Ufo the cat:

![](~/assets/images/20261015_090708.jpg)

They both work here.

Claudia only had a day in Istanbul, so we made the most of it and visited many of the cities sights. Racking in a total of 45000 steps despite the weather!

![](~/assets/images/20261015_202251.jpg)

![](~/assets/images/20261015_210512.jpg)

Claud left the following morning, hugging me and wishing me well on my travels.

![](~/assets/images/20261016_095228.jpg)

This meant I was really alone, at the start of my journey across the Asian continent. And so, I was incredibly lucky to become good friends with my fellow hostel goers.

Aksel and Stine from Norway, Simon from Montana - USA, Bayel from Kyrgyzstan, and Izel from Puerto Rico.

One night, Simon taught us how to play a card game called Cambio.

A Brazilian, a Turk, a German, an Aussie, a Kyrgyz, and a Brit walk into a bar...

Sounds like the start of a bad joke, but this was my last night in Istanbul! 

On the 5th floor of the Sanat Restaurant the six of us, having recently met, attended a trivia night. Aysha (Brazilian), Çağla (Turk), Jakob (German), Riley (Aussie), Bayel (Kyrgyz), and myself (Brit).

Surely with our combined knowledge across 6 different countries, and 4 different continents we would come first!

...

We came 10th.

My trivia knowledge leaves a lot to be desired! 😂

It was still a fantastic night which ended in some brilliant karaoke 🎤 

We even learnt the basics of belly dancing! 💃
