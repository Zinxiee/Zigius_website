From Goa, I flew to Udaipur via Delhi. But I had a strange experience with my tickets.

First, the original plane was delayed by 2.5 hours so the airline decided to change my connecting flight and, for some reason, just mine.

This meant that when I arrived at Delhi airport, everyone else connecting to Udaipur boarded their original flight, whilst I headed off to wait another 3 hours for mine. 

Then, at 6am, I received a rather panicked phone call...

"Sir, where are you? You are the last to board!"

Last to board? For a plane that wasn't due to depart for another 3 hours?

"I'm almost at gate 21 (my gate)" I replied

"Ok sir, so am I" said the operator.

But upon my arrival, no one at gate 21 was awaiting me. The departure screen showed my ticket number and confirmed that my flight was still scheduled to depart at the same time as my new booking.

I called him back.

"I'm here at gate 21"

"Where are you, Sir?"

"Gate 21"

"Please confirm your exact location Sir"

"I don't know what to tell you, I'm standing in front of a big sign that says 'Gate 21', opposite a 'Gate 22'."

"Are you downstairs Sir?"

"Downstairs? No, I'm upstairs"

"Sir you're going to be late, we are about to depart"

"My plane doesn't depart for another 3 hours!"

He appeared to ignore this.

"Sir what is your exact location"

...

It went on like this till he gave up and ended the call on me.

It turns out my original ticket had never been cancelled, and everyone but me was on that original flight, having boarded at another Gate 21 in a different terminal!

Thankfully, all was sorted in the end and I arrived in Udaipur on the next plane.
